Inspection availability depends on the machine and its location. Markers show approximate locations.

What can be checked

An inspection can cover visible condition, engine, hydraulics, tyres or undercarriage, and functional checks. Ask about diagnostics, oil sampling and other specialist tests where applicable.

What you receive

A condition report and photo evidence, with video or diagnostic data where included in the agreed scope.

How to book
